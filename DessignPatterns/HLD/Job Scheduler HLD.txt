**Functional requirement:
create a job
schedule a job that can be executed later 
Schedule a recurring job
pause/resume a job
cancel job
retry failed jobs
track status and job execution history

for now I start with one time+recurring/atleast once execution/ retries/ status and execution history and horizontal scaling

**Non functional requirements: highly available
				durable storage 
				no duplicate jobs execution 
				No scheduling delays
				Fault tolerance
				Atleast Once execution

=>Scheduler API:
Create/cancel/delete a job.
store Job metadata in DB.


=>Scheduler Worker:
find jobs where next_execution_at<=now()
publish executable jobs to kafka


=>Kafka:
decouple scheduler Service from execution.
provdes buffering during network latency.


=>Execution Worker
responsible for pulling jobs from kafka, execute actual task and updating job status.

**Why PostgreSQL?
Since we want something that is highly durable, transaction consistency and easy job management that's why PostgreSQL is the single source of truth in our design + horizontal Scaling

**Redis here does optimisation of frequently accessed scheduler info and never make redis as source of truth

**Scenarios:
Scheduler job is to poll continuously
next_execution_at<=now();

Job A-> 10am
Job B-> 10:01am

**FLOW:
	10am->job A-> scheduler claims jobA-> publishes jobA to kafka-> execution workers->job done

	then for recurring jobs
	job_completed-> calculate the next_execution_at-> update in db -> wait for next execution time

things to worry about:
	Duplicate processing since if we have multiple scheduler instances 
		Scheduler A-> JobA
		Scheduler B-> JobA
	Solution: go with distributed locks[workers acting on rows concurrently]
			skip locked[for update]
			or
			Atomic status updates
		Scheduler A-> claim JobA-> processing
		Scheudler B tries to claim same JobA-> fails[409 conflicts?]
